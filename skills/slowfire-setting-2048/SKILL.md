# Real places and 2048 daily life

Use when a scene in a 阴燃 book goes out into a real city (Chengdu, Lafayette, the Yangtze delta), when the author names places to use, or when a scene shows how people pay, shop, order food, see a doctor, get around or care for the old in 2048.

## Before writing
- Read the book's setting docs. For 火候: claude/chengdu-2048.md (places and their planned scenes, daily life) and claude/sichuan-food.md (how dishes are cooked and judged). Use what is there before searching again.
- Read the Volume brief for where scenes are already placed. In 火候 the author put the researched landmarks into Volume Two (17 Nov), not Volume One.

## Checking a place
- Search before stating anything about a real place: Chinese sources first (川观, 澎湃, 新华, 人民日报, the city's own pages), reviews only for texture.
- Record for each place: what it is, where, the founding year if it matters, one or two details a local would recognise, and any dispute (厕所串串 has two origin stories; say so). A place you can't verify waits (大蓉和).
- Real places only as places. Never attach a crime, illness, food-safety problem, scandal or bribe to a real business. A deal can be struck in a real 酒楼; a bribe-like gift stays generic.
- Real public figures are never characters (slowfire-real-people).
- Festival dates and customs by `lunardate` and a source (人日 is 正月初七, 游草堂; 元宵 偷青; 初一 and 十五 incense).

## Making the place do a job
- A place earns its line by serving the plot, a character or the book's 冷热. Otherwise cut it.
- Prefer a place that pays off a line already on the page: the 154 food line's 终点站 for 胡师傅's “坐到终点站”; 厕所串串 for 何蔓's “我不吃串串”; 文殊院 on 十五 for 邓晓燕's rosary.
- Two scenes on one festival day are fine if they set 冷 against 热 (人日: 老蒲 and 蒲嬢嬢 at 草堂 in the morning, 邝振南 at the 老店 in the afternoon).

## 2048 in one felt line
- Anchor every extrapolation to a checked 2026 fact (e-CNY earning interest, palm-vein pay, drone delivery, AI triage, long-term-care insurance, robotaxis, OTC sildenafil), then push it through the book's engines: machines are cheap and a person is the luxury (全程真人, 有个人在); motion data is sold; bodies are rented; everyone wears glasses.
- One line per place in the prose, best as an object or a price (a menu with two prices, 机炒 and 人炒). Never a paragraph of explanation.
- Keep to what the text has fixed. For 火候: driverless cars with no wheel, called in the glasses; the metro is the backbone, pooled driverless minibuses stop at the old poles, heritage lines keep a driver (胡师傅 on the 154 one day a week, his voice on its announcements); 天府's fourth floor sells old work as leisure; its top floor sells rooms, not films (片子不要钱，房间要钱; 不出房间，就不算传播); 长护险 hours in three grades (machine, remote-driven body, 真人上门).

## Food
- Dish facts from the food doc: 回锅肉 (二刀, about two minutes on fierce fire, 灯盏窝, 郫县豆瓣, crisp 蒜苗); 宫保 (leg meat, 糊辣荔枝味, sauce that clings); 干烧 (no starch, reduced by fire); 腰花 (麦穗花刀, seconds).
- How Chengdu judges: flat slices fail; dice that don't wear their sauce fail; a plate should end with only a little oil; a 馆子 with no queue is suspect.
- Picky lines go in the speaker's dialect (sichuanese-dialogue) and cut both ways between Chengdu and Chongqing (roast-in-fiction). The narrator never lectures about authenticity.

## Record
- Add each checked place to the setting doc's table (place | what is real | 2048 line | fits) with its source, and each placement to the Volume brief.
- Famous places get no 注 (slowfire-chapter-notes calibration). Figures show places without legible signs (slowfire-figures).
