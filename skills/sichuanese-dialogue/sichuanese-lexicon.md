# 四川话 lexicon for 火候 (and any Sichuan dialogue in 阴燃)

Settled lines and words, so a known line needs no Qwen round. Columns: the form, what it does, who says it, and the source (author, Qwen round, or canon chapter). Add a row whenever the author or a Qwen round settles something. Rejected forms stay listed so nobody reintroduces them. Bare chapter numbers (ch28) are 阴燃 Volume Two; 火候 chapters are marked 火候 chN. The house standard for spelling and registers is claude/chengdu-standard.md.

**Who to ask (18 Nov).** When a Chengdu form is unclear, ask the author; the author's word outranks Qwen and every source. For 南充 / 西充 speech and customs, always ask the author and do not settle them from sources. Research scope: only the book's regions (Chengdu proper, 郫都 and the country districts, 南充 / 川北, Chongqing as the outside voice). The 18 Nov rows marked "round one" or "round two" come from two research reports sent in chat that day (地道成都话与老成都生活; 成都话原声与老成都日常).

## Keep out of 玉林 mouths
- **Chongqing only:** sentence-final 吔; brusque 各人出去 (各人 itself is Chengdu too); 嘞 (= 这) / 咾 / 哩; 啷个.
- **都江堰 河西 only:** 太阳包儿, 月儿光, 卷人 (scold). Nobody in the cast is from there.
- **Outer districts:** retroflex sounds (郫都, 新都, 华阳, 崇州…), per natives. Narration only, for 二嬢's village.
- **李劼人's era, quote only:** 不曾 (tag), 罢, 下子, 哩 (topic), 顶 (= 最), 哥子.
- **Other regions, not for this cast (author's scope, 18 Nov):** 达州's passive 着, the 南路 -子 forms (滚水, 火提子), 川南, 川西, 青川 / 广元.

## Rejected (do not use)
- 错得板正: no Chengdu speaker says it (author).
- 不是扇风，是扇时间: an author's 金句 in a teahouse boss's mouth (author and Qwen).
- 禁令下来了 for 判了: wrong, because it's a court ruling (ch2).
- 扎起 for tucking a shirt in: its first meaning is 撑腰 (back someone up). Write 把衣裳扎到裤腰里 / 扎好 (ch28, Qwen).
- 哟 (bare) in a Chengdu mouth: Beijing speech (author 18 Nov; Qwen ch28). Use 哦哟, or 哟喂 for mock dismay at oneself. 火候 ch19 fixed to 哦哟 (18 Nov). 铁子's 东北 哟 is his own.
- 吹壳子: sounds off to the author (18 Nov). Seen online; the book says 冲壳子.
- 耙耳朵 as a picture of Chengdu couples, and 屋头婆娘说了算 (author 18 Nov; the ch4 line was replaced).
- The drawled 梅花音 as the mark of the oldest natives: a 1970s street accent that later passed for "real" Chengdu (author 18 Nov).
- 噶 / 嘎 as an old man's interjection: suspect (Yunnan-leaning); don't use.
- 瓜批, 日龙宝 and the 批 family, 妈卖批, 仙人板板: off the page.
- 我的刀，我说了算 (老蒲): a poster line, 金句 (Qwen 17 Nov). He answers with an act instead (ch29).
- 火候是听出来的，不是算出来的 (老蒲, 火候 ch8): too neat, a slogan (Qwen 17 Nov). Card laid: 火候是耳朵听出来的，你拿脑壳算啥子？
- 手是别人的，力气是别人的，砧板是隔了几千公里的 (老蒲 ch27): a tricolon 金句; card laid: 那是别个的手。/ 这是你自己的。(18 Nov pass)
- 走这么早？ (汪蓉): plain Mandarin (Qwen 17 Nov). Use 这早就走嗦？
- 站口 for a bus stop (胡师傅): not said (Qwen 17 Nov). Use 哪一站.
- 灶上头 for "at the stove": reads as "on top of the stove" (Qwen 17 Nov). Use 灶头.
- 石沉大海 in a 29-year-old's mouth: too bookish (Qwen r2). Use 打水漂.
- 平台不得让你不要: a tangled double negative (Qwen r2). Use 平台不得准.
- 投着耍: use the settled 投起耍的.
- 拉不起来 (汪蓉): Mandarin; use 扯不起你 (Qwen r3).
- 起身走了 (徐好翔): bookish; use 站起来走咯 (Qwen r3).
- 名字留到 (Chongqing wife): too Mandarin; she says 招牌不换 (Qwen r3).
- 啷个 in a Chengdu mouth (老蒲): Chongqing; use 咋个 (Qwen r8).
- 他二姨爹 between adults: the child's term; use 我妹夫 / 你妹夫 (Qwen r7).
- 姨公: use 姨爷爷 (Qwen r7).
- 站到起: odd (Qwen r7). 起火 for lighting the stove: ambiguous (a fire); use 生火 (Qwen r7).
- 呢 in a Chengdu native's question: use 喃 (house standard; 18 Nov pass).
- 想了一哈 in narration: narration is Mandarin (想了一下); 一哈 belongs in dialogue (slip caught in ch42, 18 Nov).
- Dialect in narration: not taken. A Chengdu POV may carry a light free-indirect touch (一张纸上啥子都写了，就是没得一栏写); Lafayette sections never.
- Americans (Amish included) never end a question with 不？ (阿莫斯, ch29 fixed to 吗).

## Disputed (decide case by case)
- 男娃儿 for a grown man. Qwen says it means a little boy. Locals do use it for young men, but in 蒲嬢嬢's coming-out line ch4 uses 是个男的, which lands harder.
- 得不得行 (行不行): Qwen r2 called it ungrammatical and offered 得行不. It is common Chengdu speech; kept in 汪蓉's 写朋友得不得行？(ch34).
- 盖到 as an imperative (keep covered): Qwen r2 preferred 盖好嘛. Kept for 汪蓉, who says one thing once (ch34), with 莫着凉 after it.
- 扎不起 (can't tie): Qwen r2 read it as "can't afford"; changed to 扎不起来 in 张磊's line (ch33) to avoid the misreading.
- 背后头 (behind someone's back): Qwen r2 preferred 背地里. Kept for 王芳 (ch36).
- 灶是你的 (蒲嬢嬢, ch32): Qwen r2 preferred 灶头归你用. Kept: in a divorce the plain ownership claim is the point.
- 猪来穷，狗来富，猫来披孝布 (徐好翔 ch39): Qwen r3 flagged it. The author doesn't know it either (18 Nov). The well-known saying is 猫来穷，狗来富; the long form lives in short-video folklore and is not tied to Chengdu, so Qwen's flag was right. The fix belongs to the Volume Two chat: the common form, or keep 披孝布 and let someone question it (你从哪儿听来的？) so it reads as 徐好翔's own lore and the omen for 钱维岳 survives.
- 抱到雪地上 (汪蓉 ch39): Qwen r3 preferred 弄到雪地上. Kept 抱, because it answers 徐好翔's "你把它抱到别处去" about the cat.
- 锁起的，也是给你锁的 (钟姐 火候 ch5): Qwen 17 Nov preferred 就是给你锁的 and dropping 她 from 不给你锁，她锁啥子. Kept 也 (the paradox) and 她 (it names 邓晓燕).
- 焯了一道水 (老蒲's narration, ch44): Qwen r8 called it stiff; changed to 焯了水 though 焯一道水 is common in kitchens.

## Speakers and register
- **The Chengdu manner (author, 18 Nov): 幽默 but 委婉, and they 编故事 a lot.** The point comes sideways: wrapped in a joke, a made-up anecdote, a question back, never a flat statement. Criticism, refusal and affection all arrive wrapped. Tall tales and invented stories are ordinary talk (冲壳子, 日白, 打胡乱说), told with a straight face; the listener knows and plays along (a native online: 他随便说你随便听就是了，不要当真). It is currency between people, not lying to deceive. A Chengdu line that states the point outright is usually wrong.
- **Couples (author, 18 Nov):** neither serves the other; each keeps their own say, nobody waits on anybody. Old couples banter as equals, and a comeback can throw the other's own word back. 耙耳朵 is an outsider's exaggeration: little of it, and answered.
- **The 2048 elders (born in the 1980s):** their speech is today's forty-year-olds', not their parents'. Real elders' lines are plain, one dialect marker a line at the front or the end; it thins at the clinic, with officials and on the phone, and thickens at the table (round two).
- **成都婆罗门 (author, 18 Nov):** an old inner-city family. Natives say it of themselves as a joke; outsiders say it as a jab. They roast 外地人 but never look down on them; often it's the opposite. Banter, never contempt or snobbery.
- **蒲嬢嬢, 老蒲 (60s, 玉林):** full particles; 嬢嬢, 婆娘, 老汉儿; blunt, self-mocking. 老蒲 never wins on a punchline; he answers with his hands. No poster lines. 老蒲 says 咋个, never 啷个. 懂得起 and 亮膘 fit them; 吃回锅肉 (redo a job) and 开茶钱 don't (author 18 Nov).
- **二嬢 (60, 郫都, 蒲嬢嬢's younger sister):** country Chengdu, sharp-tongued, calls 老蒲 姐夫 even mid-divorce; 远个铲铲, 整二十桌; her toast 城里头吃不到这么相因的; 苗苗喃？/ 人喃？
- **徐好翔, 汪蓉:** lazy, ironic, short. 汪蓉: 一句是一句.
- **邱野, 唐蕾 (around 30):** light dialect, 骚话, quick; no 成语.
- **邓晓燕 (48, ICU nurse):** full at home, brisk; 喃, 屋头, 病房头, 没得; numbers and drugs in Mandarin terms.
- **老罗 (62, 南充, decades in Chengdu):** 川北 tinge: 女子 for daughter; 莫多心, 一码归一码; shirt always tucked. 没得 in Chengdu, 莫得 slipping in (author 18 Nov). The 花椒 bath (火候 ch10) is his own habit, not a 南充 custom (author).
- **罗佳 (34, vet, Chengdu-born):** light to medium: 喃, 晓不晓得; speaks to 邓晓燕 directly, no 成语.
- **苗苗 (7, raised in 上海 by Chengdu parents):** light Chengdu over Mandarin: 姨婆, 姨爷爷, 藏到这儿, 偷来的. Never near any sex thread.
- **何蔓她爸 (58, a 西充 village in 川北; author 18 Nov):** few words; 灶屋, 走嘛; 莫得 at home. 西充 speech is crisp, every word fully pronounced ("like British English", the author), hence 一个字一个字往外蹦. First named in 火候 ch2 (川北西充的口音, 18 Nov). The village sits in hills, not mountains: 坡, 沟, 坡地, never 山 (火候 ch2 and ch16 fixed 18 Nov). 腊猪脚 on 除夕 is right. For anything else about 西充, ask the author.
- **胡师傅 (46, bus driver turned instructor):** full; bus words (哪一站, 起步, 打灯, 到站了说一声哈).
- **何蔓:** cutting, mostly Mandarin with a Chengdu edge.
- **王鹏:** insists on Mandarin and mispronounces confidently (恪 as gé). Sichuanese only when it matters: to 老邓 (ch27), to 何蔓 (要得, ch30), to 钟姐 on request (ch35), and the truth to 张一帆 (ch36, "他说的是成都话"). When the narration says he spoke pure Mandarin, his line carries no 啥子 or 屋头 (ch25 fixed 18 Nov).
- **王芳 (40, courier station):** full Chengdu, fast, counts who paid for what (房租哪个交的？奶粉哪个买的？); never says the truth herself, sends it to her brother.
- **张一帆 (15):** short questions, Chengdu particles light; mocks his uncle's memes (包浆了).
- **钟姐 (55):** Chengdu, money-minded, 嘛 when she gives an order.
- **蒲青:** Mandarin in Lafayette; texts his father's people in Chengdu speech; with his mother, 装的啥子.
- **Chongqing speakers:** harder and louder; 老子; 啷个; 吔, 嘞; tease Chengdu as 耙耳朵 and get answered (蒲嬢嬢 火候 ch4). 假打 is Chengdu's own word, not their jab (round one). Greeting on the phone: 过年好哈 (not 嘛; ch28, Qwen); 对头 and the run-on 授权了的，手印都按了的 are right. The boss's wife (40s, runs the kitchen, few words): 腰花要得。回锅肉，肉没卷起。干烧鱼，糖放重了。/ 招牌不换，味道我们自己调。(ch39, Qwen r3).
- **铁子 (38, 沈阳铁西, years in Chengdu; Qwen round 17 Nov):** 嗷 at most once a line and mostly as an answer or a flourish; a string of 嗷 is short-video 东北, not 铁西. 指定, 唠个嗑, 为啥不, 爷俩, 跟商量好的似的, 正经货, X啥呀X are right (它敲啥呢？ch39). Let Chengdu bleed in now and then (嘛, 晓得, 要得), which shows his years here (Qwen r2 objected to his 嘛; not taken). Fixed: 图的就是有人陪唠，手谁没有啊 (ch26); 再浅就翻不起花了 (ch29); 今儿个，一个没切穿嗷 (ch35, Qwen r2: 没断 was ambiguous).
- **乐子:** Hangzhou Mandarin (我知道, 买什么); after ten years with 邓晓燕 he answers her once in her word (我晓得的, 火候 ch21). 杭州 forms: claude/delta-lexicon.md.
- **徐州 old man (ch28):** 中原官话, not 江淮: 上丰县？/ 上丰县干啥？ (上 for 去).
- **季大有 (61, 丰县, 火德 canon):** 恁 / 俺 kept (canon voice; Qwen r2 thought 恁 too formal for a peer, not taken); 好些没, 上哪去 (Qwen r2). His tenet 天让恁看见什么，就是叫恁去办什么 is canon, never reworded.
