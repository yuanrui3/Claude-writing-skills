# Chapter pace and braid

The author's rules (火候, 17 Nov):

- push faster, and spend detail only on relationships;
- every chapter moves at least one relationship by a real event on the page;
- end on a hook;
- spread the big turns out;
- 3,500 to 4,500 汉字 a chapter.

## Beat plan (scratch, before drafting)

Give each section one line: the clock or clocks in its heading, the POV, the relationship, the event (a kiss, a bed, a confession, a break, a discovery, a line crossed), what changes, and the last line.

- At least one section carries a real event; the others may seed.
- When uninhibited adults are in the chapter, at least one sex scene is on the page (intimate-scenes).
- Each seed names the chapter that pays it off.

## Braiding

- Four to seven sections, in rotating close third.
- Headings carry both clocks and never run backwards. Sections may overlap in time.
- Cut for contrast: set a free scene beside a repressed one (唐蕾 and 邱野 come between 王鹏 deleting his message and 乐子 on the recliner).
- Echo across sections with an object or a word (the buttons in ch6), never with commentary.
- A section with a child in it never holds a sex scene.

## Detail budget

- After drafting, mark each paragraph R (relationship), W (world or logistics) or X (anything else). If W and X together pass a quarter of the chapter, cut each to one line.
- No paragraphs of arriving and leaving unless the walk is the scene.
- Count 汉字 by script.

## Endings

- The chapter's last line is a turn, a withheld reaction, or an image that reframes the chapter (Corinne's crooked apple; Ines knowing who took over). No summary and no moral.
- Each section's last line already pulls toward the next one.

## After drafting

- Optionally, a Qwen read in the pacing chat, with sensitive premises blurred.
- For key chapters, run plot-unexpectedness to test how predictable they are.
- Log each chapter's events in the index, so the turns stay spread out.
