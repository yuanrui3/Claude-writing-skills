# Net-speak in dialogue

The author wants some characters to talk the way today's Chinese internet talks. In Chengdu that is 抽象 culture (孙笑川 and 抽象工作室, rooted in 四川方言). One northern character carries 东北整活 (东百往事: 虎哥, 刀哥).

## Source of truth

- The project doc `claude/netspeak-lexicon.md` holds each form with its meaning, register, speaker, and what is never used. Read it before writing.
- Add a form only after checking it: Moegirl or a news explainer for the meaning and the year, or the author's word. Record the source. Mark an unverified origin as unverified.
- No existing skill covered this (searched 17 Nov 2026).

## The 2048 frame

By 2048 every form in the lexicon is 20 to 30 years old, and it dates the speaker the way any slang does a quarter-century on.

- Speakers in their 30s and 40s use it by habit or with irony.
- Teenagers hear it as 老登 talk (张一帆: “包浆了”).
- Don't invent 2048 slang wholesale. One coined word a book at most, and only in a teenager's mouth.

## Who speaks it (火候)

- **王鹏:** 抽象 in typed work chat (典, 急, 宁配吗, 😅). His 错别字 are real typos, never jokes. Spoken, he insists on Mandarin, so it's lighter there.
- **唐蕾:** a 乐, a 绷不住了, a 我佛辣. Laughter words only.
- **宋铁军 (铁子):** a 后屋 operator from 沈阳铁西. 东北整活 spoken cadence (嗷, 指定, 好果汁吃, mock ceremonies). His 整活 is how he performs toughness far from home.
- **何蔓:** at most a 差不多得了.
- **张一帆:** mocks his uncle's memes.
- Nobody in Lafayette, and never the narration.

## How to use it

1. **Dose.** One or two forms in a scene. A whole line of memes only when that is the joke (王鹏's chat post).
2. **It must reveal something:** armour (王鹏), performance (铁子), generation (张一帆), or a way of not saying the real thing. If it's decoration, cut it.
3. **Typed or spoken.** Emoji and initialisms belong to typed chat. 嗷 and 指定 belong to speech. Spoken 抽象 from a Chengdu mouth carries Chengdu sounds (sichuanese-dialogue).
4. **Banter goes both ways.** 王鹏 against 铁子, and every jab gets a comeback (the regional-banter rule).
5. **Real people.** 孙笑川, 虎哥, 刀哥 and the singer behind 泰裤辣 are never characters. They appear only as old clips that someone quotes or plays, with no claims about their lives.
6. **Never:**
   - 火星文, or 谐音梗 烂活: emoji or look-alike characters swapped in for words (🐮🍺, 🐴 for 妈), puns built on sound (蚌埠住了, 带哥), typos played as jokes. The catchphrases the author named (泰裤辣, 我佛辣, 宁配吗) stay, quoted whole;
   - insults about anyone's parents (NMSL and the like);
   - slurs, including the 🍺-for-屄 trick used as an insult;
   - 牢大, which mocks a dead man;
   - self-harm 狠活;
   - jokes about fights, arrests or scams tied to real people.
7. **Notes.** Add a ① note only when a reader who isn't online couldn't get the line from context (slowfire-chapter-notes). A meme explained in the next line needs no note.
