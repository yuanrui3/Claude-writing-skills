# Imagery and texture

The author flagged unnatural 比喻 (扇时间) and wants world detail only through what a character feels, and briefly. The images that worked came from the character's own trade:

- 邱野 freezes like a body in the 后屋 when its link drops;
- 唐蕾 pats his chest like laying down a tile;
- Corinne counts out the sex like pills.

## The 比喻 audit

Run it on every 像, 如, 似, 仿佛 and 一样 in a chapter, and on every implied metaphor.

1. **Whose eyes?** Name the POV character.
2. **Where does the image come from?** It must come from their work, place, era or body: mahjong, the pharmacy, the wok, the operator floor, the Amish barn. An image from the writer's library (literary, abstract, a Western idiom) is replaced or cut.
3. **How many?** One a paragraph at most. None in dialogue unless that speaker would say it.
4. **Stock images.** No 网文 stock (心如刀割, 眼神如刀) and no AI stock (像一盏灯, 像一首诗).
5. **By script.** List every 像…一样 and 像…似的 with its POV character, then look at the outsiders.
6. **When unsure,** ask Qwen whether a native reader finds it natural (qwen-consult).

## World texture: the future in passing

- The future appears as an object or habit someone handles: the line of text in the corner of the glasses, the Cradle flyer on the fridge, an operator's steady hands. One line, inside an action or a feeling.
- Never a paragraph of mechanism. Explain only when a character needs it right then, in their own words.
- Numbers appear only when a character counts them.
- A texture line still has to be true: check it against the canon in the index doc.
