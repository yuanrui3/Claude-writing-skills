# Roasts inside a novel

The roast-anything skill writes stand-up. A novel needs the same blade held by a character, in a scene that changes because of it. Load roast-anything for its core rule, devices and guardrails, then apply the changes below.

## When to use it
- A sharp-tongued character speaks: 蒲嬢嬢 at the 相亲角 ("直爽就是吼人嘛"), 何蔓 on 王鹏, 唐蕾, 乐子, 萨凡纳's sales patter.
- A quarrel, a work-chat fight, a toast at the 年夜饭, a Chengdu–Chongqing 互怼 (the hotpot boss: 成都的回锅肉甜咪咪的; 蒲青's reply).
- Satire of the book's own institutions: template-body showrooms, Cradle, 天府's top floor, the Steward (管家), the ban, the 相亲角's AI-polished résumés, in-world op-eds and comment sections.
- The narrator's dry aside, one line, sparingly.

## What changes from the stage

**Facts.**
- The facts come from the book: the Map, the plot ledgers, earlier chapters. A callback the reader can check beats a new fact.
- A real-world fact only when that character would know it; verify it as roast-anything says.
- No Sources list; log any new in-world fact in the Map.

**Voice.**
- The roast belongs to a character: their vocabulary, dialect, age, job and what they know (check the intel ledger).
- 四川话 lines go to Qwen.
- No 大小姐 persona unless the character is an AI.

**Stakes.**
- In fiction a roast is an action. It shifts who has the upper hand, and someone answers: a comeback, a laugh, or a silence that shows something.
- Nobody is only a punching bag. The roaster gets cut back somewhere in the book (the turn).

**Self-image.**
- Keep the core rule, 谁最不许被调侃，谁先被调侃.
- Write the target's self-image in one scratch line, then find the in-world behaviour that contradicts it.

**Rhythm on the page.**
- No （停一拍） or 【笑】 cues. Use a paragraph break, a reaction beat or a short line instead.
- Never explain the joke, and the narrator never laughs at it.

**Devices that carry over.**
- Refusal as roast.
- Roast the roasters.
- The fact as punchline.
- Jargon mapping: into the world's own terms, such as 第三档套餐, 模板身子, 值守, 验证人类.
- Callbacks across chapters.
- 成语 and 俗语 where the speaker would use them.

## Guardrails
Keep all of roast-anything's guardrails, plus the book's:
- No ethnic group is roasted. Regional 互怼 is mutual banter that lands both ways.
- No suicide jokes.
- Children are never targets.
- Real public figures appear only as objects, never with invented lines; recognisable real people never.
- Victims stay out.
- A character may say something bigoted only if the scene answers it and the narration doesn't endorse it.

## Workflow
1. Pick the moment and the roaster. Ask: what does the scene need this roast to change?
2. One scratch line on the target's self-image. Pull 2 or 3 facts from the Map, the ledgers or earlier chapters.
3. Write 2 to 4 escalating beats ending on a turn: back at the roaster, at the listener, or at the institution behind both.
4. Write the reaction beat.
5. Run slowfire-zh-check, send dialect lines to Qwen, and log new facts.
6. Report to the author in one line where the bit sits and what it calls back to.
