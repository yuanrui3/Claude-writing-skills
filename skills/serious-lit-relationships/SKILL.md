# Relationships as serious fiction writes them

The author wants the novels (阴燃 and its spin-offs, 火候 first) to read as 严肃文学, with more novelty than a 言情小说. A list of admired authors doesn't change a draft. A move you can point to on the page does.

## The move table

Keep it in the book's index doc, under "Craft". Each row gives the work, its move, the thread it serves, and a test on the page. The starting rows (火候, 17 Nov):

| Work | Move | Test on the page |
|---|---|---|
| 金宇澄《繁花》 | 不响: the turn is a silence; talk at tables | the scene's key answer is a non-answer |
| 王小波《黄金时代》 | sex that is frank, comic, argued over and unashamed | someone jokes in bed, and the joke shows who they are |
| 谷崎润一郎《痴人之爱》《春琴抄》 | power flips over time; a fetish works as devotion | whoever starts on top isn't on top at the end |
| 杜拉斯《情人》 | money in the room; the aftermath outweighs the act | the scene ends on the after |
| 张爱玲 (《色，戒》, the stories) | the cool eye; one object carries the relationship; desire changes the plot | name the object; cut the scene and the plot breaks |
| 苏童《妻妾成群》 | the household's order decides who sleeps with whom | a seating, rota or room assignment means something |
| 贾平凹《废都》 (□□□) | the blank as a device | blanks land where the reader most wants the scene |
| Alice Munro | a later vantage shows what a moment meant | one flash across years |
| Raymond Carver | couples talk around the thing | the thing is never named in the dialogue |
| Annie Ernaux《简单的激情》 | desire recorded flatly, time measured in waiting | no moral, no adjectives of feeling |
| Elena Ferrante | love mixed with envy and debt | someone keeps count |
| Sally Rooney | power and class inside dialogue; consent spoken aloud | who pays and who decides, said out loud |
| Milan Kundera | lightness against weight; eros split from love; each lover's code | the character's rule is stated or broken |
| 毕飞宇《推拿》 | touch as work; the dignity of rented hands | hands do a job in the scene |
| 霍达《穆斯林的葬礼》 | a craft as a whole life, passed from master to apprentice; sisters and a child raised by the wrong one in secret; faith as the wall love runs into, written from inside with respect; ritual and death written concretely and solemnly; two time strands braided | the craft carries the feeling; a secret about who raised whom; a rite shown step by step, never mocked |

To add a work, it must be one the author named or a canonical book you know well. Write its move and test in your own words, and never quote or paste passages from it. A craft essay found by web search can sharpen a row; cite it in the index.

## Each chapter

1. **Plan.** In the scratch plan, give each relationship beat one line: who, what shifts between them, and which move carries it.
2. **Draft.** Relationship detail gets the words; logistics get the minimum. The author's rule: push faster, and spend detail only on relationships.
3. **Test.** Check each beat against its row's test. A beat with no move is rewritten or cut.
4. **Grep for 言情 and 网文 tells, then fix them.**
   - Feelings stated outright (他终于明白, 心头一暖, 爱上了).
   - A fated couple, flawless bodies, chiselled jaws.
   - 霸总 gestures (捏下巴, 壁咚, 邪魅一笑).
   - Dragged-out misunderstandings, villains, love resolved at the end.
   The anti-言情 rules: no destined couple and no villain; love tangled with money and housing; desire fixes nothing; endings stay open.
5. **Pastiche drill (optional, for a key scene).** Draft it in the manner of two different rows, then keep what fits the characters, never the imitation itself.
6. **Outside read.** Qwen can say where a non-sex scene slips into a 网文 register (qwen-consult). Never send it sex scenes.
7. **Log.** Record in the index which moves each chapter used, so the book doesn't lean on one.
